SimpleScript: Free Screenwriting App

Write screenplays in your browser with industry-standard formatting. SimpleScript opens and saves Final Draft (.fdx) files, exports PDF and keeps working offline after your first visit. Free, with no account and nothing to install.

Features

  • Screenplay formatting as you type: scene headings, action, characters, dialogue, parentheticals and transitions
  • Open, edit and save Final Draft (.fdx) files
  • Export to PDF, import and export Fountain, import PDF scripts
  • Title page, scene list and Beat Board
  • Drafts saved in your browser, works offline after the first visit
  • Spell check, light and dark themes

Made by Onur Erdurak (Blendreams), a director and VFX artist in Los Angeles. The desktop app is free too: SimpleScript on Gumroad.
